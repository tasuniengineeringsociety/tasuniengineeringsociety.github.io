How It Works

1

Pick your dates

Click your start and end dates on the availability calendar. Each day shows how many panels are still free, so you can see clashes before they happen.

2

Get an instant quote

Choose your panel count and delivery option — the price updates live as you go. No surprises, and the refundable bond is shown up front.

3

Request your booking

Submit the form and you'll get a reference number and confirmation email straight away. The committee reviews and confirms every booking, usually within 2 business days.
